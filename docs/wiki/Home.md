# ShekelSync

**Your Israeli banks, credit cards, and financial plans, together on your computer.**

ShekelSync is an open-source desktop app for understanding your spending, reviewing transactions, and planning future cash flow. It connects to supported Israeli financial institutions and stores your financial records in a local SQLite database.

## Local data, optional integrations

Your transactions and financial plans stay on your device. Bank credentials are encrypted using a key protected by your OS keychain. The financial database itself is not encrypted at rest. Optional AI, Telegram, donations, and opt-in usage statistics have their own data flows; read [Privacy and data](Privacy-and-Data) before enabling them.
