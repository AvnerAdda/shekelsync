# Features

The wiki describes the current project. Some development features may arrive in the next release; check the [changelog](https://github.com/AvnerAdda/shekelsync/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for your installed version.

## Home: understand the overview

Home brings income, expenses, investments, and cash flow together. Choose a period to explore your financial history. Use the quick amount-hiding control when sharing your screen; **Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + H** toggles it from the keyboard.

**Projection** shows expected balances over a selectable horizon, before or after investing. Confidence tiers distinguish sure, likely, and uncertain flows. Saved planning changes can also be included in the outlook.

**Review all** opens Money Review for items needing attention.

## Activity: review the records behind the totals

Browse, search, filter, and categorize imported transactions. Check categories and credit-card payment matching before interpreting spending totals, especially after adding a new institution.

## Plan: decide what comes next

### Spending categories

The timeline compares spending categories with income over rolling 30-, 60-, or 90-day windows. Each date has its own trailing window. Unspent income is allocated to Growth; spending above income shows a deficit. Category targets provide reference lines. Changing the displayed history does not change the rolling-window length.

### Available to spend

This estimate accounts for expected spending, outstanding card commitments, savings-goal reserves, and a cash buffer before the next expected income. Income dates and card commitments are detected from imported data and forecasts. When a reliable payday is unavailable, the estimate uses a rolling 30-day period.

Use **Adjust** for optional overrides. The app labels fallback spending estimates and missing information; it does not invent a missing balance.

### Goals and scenarios

Save goals with target dates, amounts already saved, and monthly contributions. Specify whether existing savings are inside the included bank balance or held elsewhere. Goal allocations are local planning records and do not transfer money.

What-if scenarios compare one-time or monthly cash changes with your current plan. They do not alter transaction history or the main forecast.

### Budgets

Track category budgets and inspect forecast risk alongside recent spending. Budget warnings and forecasts depend on your imported data being complete and up to date.

## Wealth: investments, debt, and property

- **Investments:** view recorded accounts and portfolio values.
- **Debt repayment planner:** compare existing payments with extra payments directed to the highest-interest or smallest-balance debt. Schedules are separated by currency and assume fixed rates, monthly interest, and no new borrowing or fees.
- **Real estate:** model properties and multi-track mortgages, drawdowns, prepayments, and payment reconciliation. Explore repayment schedules, stress scenarios, refinancing, and sale break-even estimates using your assumptions and costs.

## Settings and optional services

Manage account connections, appearance, local data, and privacy settings. The AI assistant requires your own provider API key and is optional. Telegram and anonymous usage statistics are also optional. See [Privacy and data](Privacy-and-Data) for what can leave your device.
